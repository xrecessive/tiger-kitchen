# Traveling Tiger Center — Kitchen Chaos

Cook the dinner rush, repair failing equipment, chase an escaping Raja, and
keep a baby out of trouble. Everything is generated procedurally in Godot.

Play it: https://xrecessive.github.io/tiger-kitchen/

WASD moves, E interacts, hold E to chop/cook/repair/scare, Esc pauses, and F11
toggles fullscreen.
